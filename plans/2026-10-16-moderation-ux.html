Hide, ban and remove dialogs

The current dialogs, a list of all changes, then the proposed dialogs. A ban hides a member's messages for everyone, and the member is not told. Removal of a banned member, and "Delete and ban" for a contact or customer, block coming back via the link or address for the duration of the ban: 1 day, 1 week or forever. Sections tagged "current" show today's dialogs; every other tag marks a proposal. The drawings are schematic.

Why it is insufficient

  • Nothing stops a return. Removal and deletion leave the group link and the address usable by the same person.
  • "Block for all" is used in place of removal, because it is the only way to keep a spammer from rejoining. The spammer stays connected to every member, and all members see the block.
  • Nothing is timed. A cool-down needs a manual unblock or a second role change.
  • "Block" means "ignore". Users expect a blocked person to stop seeing their messages.
  • Business chats have no tool against an abusive customer.
  • "Only delete conversation" archives the contact and clears the conversation in one action. Users asked for each separately.
  • The contact deletion dialogs offer every combination: conversation or contact, keep the conversation or delete it, notify or stay silent. The outcome is stated nowhere before it happens.

The proposal

The proposed model

  • Ban hides messages. The banned member is not told. In groups nothing is sent to their app; in channels the relays send the ban to all subscribers, the banned subscriber included. Admins and moderators see placeholders for the member's messages, as today; other members see nothing. Members using older app versions see placeholders, as today. Hiding is applied by the other members' apps and by relays.
  • Member bans last 1 day, 1 week or forever. The default is 1 day. While the member stays, a forever ban lasts until lifted.
  • Removing a banned member is silent and blocks rejoining. Other members see no event (members using app versions without this change see the removal), and the removed person is not told. The person can't rejoin via the group link for the duration of the ban, counted from the removal; after a forever ban, never.
  • Banning and removing members takes one dialog: the duration and the action are chosen together. Only a removal with a forever ban adds a second dialog. Deleting a contact, a group or a business chat takes two dialogs.
  • Removal with a forever ban needs a typed confirmation. The dialog explains that the block on rejoining can't be lifted, and that it is stored only in the person's app, so the person can still join from another device or after reinstalling the app; "yes" (not translated) has to be typed. A ban without removal asks for no typing: it sets no block on rejoining and can be lifted.
  • Removing a member who is not banned stays as today: visible to everyone, and rejoining is not blocked.
  • "Delete and ban" for a contact or customer: deleted without notification, and can't connect via your address for 1 day, 1 week or forever; the default is 1 day. It can't be lifted. A new one-time link is not affected by it; for a customer, such a link creates a contact, not a business chat.
  • Removal of a banned member always sets a ban. The removal dialog offers 1 day, 1 week and forever, with 1 day as the default, also in bulk removal. When no selected member is banned, removal stays as today.
  • The block on rejoining or reconnecting is applied by the removed person's own app. It is stored only in the app's database, so removing the app ends it. Storage that survives removal of the app is not used: a record left on a device after SimpleX is deleted would let an admin plant a marker that identifies the group on a seized phone.
  • Archive, Clear, Delete and Ban are separate chat list actions for a contact. Archive keeps the conversation; Clear keeps the chat in the list.
  • A business chat is deleted like a contact on the owner's side: the same actions, "Keep conversation", and a confirmation. Unlike a contact, the customer is always notified by "Delete"; only "Delete and ban" is silent. Leaving a group or a business chat, and deleting it after leaving, stay as today.
  • An owner can keep the conversation when deleting a group or channel. Today's alert is followed by a second dialog with "Keep conversation"; with it on, the owner's chat stays in the list as "group deleted".
  • Only admins and moderators see bans, with the event and the placeholders as today. Regular members see no event or placeholder.

Version rules

  • Bans of every duration work with every app version. Newer apps end a timed ban at its end time; for older apps, the app that set the ban sends the message that lifts it when the ban ends.
  • The block on rejoining or reconnecting is applied only by apps that support it (protocol version 21). "Ban and remove", and the duration choice when a banned member is removed, are offered only when the member's app supports it; otherwise removal is as today. "Delete and ban" is shown only when the app of the contact or customer supports it, and for a contact only when they connected via your address.

Protocol

  • A ban uses the existing x.grp.mem.restrict, which moderators can already send, with an optional end time. It is not sent to the banned member, as today.
  • When a timed ban ends, the app that set it sends x.grp.mem.restrict lifting it, for apps that can't apply the end time.
  • The block on rejoining or reconnecting is included in the removal or deletion message: x.grp.mem.del, x.grp.del for business chats, x.direct.del. Its duration is counted from the server time of that message: the chosen duration, with no end time for a forever ban.
  • A silent removal or deletion sends the same message with the silent flag: the app stores the block and shows nothing.
  • Deletion of a group, channel or business chat by its owner gets "Keep conversation", through the delete mode that APIDeleteChat already takes and ignores for groups. The owner's chat is kept in the state that a member has today after receiving x.grp.del.
  • The reason field is dropped.

Unchanged

  • A chat belongs to at most one list.
  • Leaving a group, channel or business chat, and deleting it after leaving.
  • Removal of a member who is not banned.
  • Removal of an invited member who has not joined: deleted locally, nothing is sent.
  • Rejecting and accepting a pending member. A pending member can also be banned from the member menu.
  • Changing a member's role.
  • Clearing a chat: "Clear conversation?" for contacts and groups.
  • Removing a relay (channels).
  • Deleting a contact that is not ready or already deleted: "Confirm contact deletion?" with Confirm.
  • Archive for groups and business chats: not part of this change.
