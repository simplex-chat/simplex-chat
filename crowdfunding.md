## AI increases demand for privacy

Surveillance, deplatforming, and online fraud used to require human effort, so they were relatively rare — and most people thought they needed no protection.*

AI removed the effort: it scans messages at scale, deplatforms automatically, and can fake any face, voice, or number. Anyone with a user identifier will now be targeted — so everyone will want protection.

AI agents handling credentials and money increase demand further: an agent whose identity is controlled by a platform can be shut down or turned against its owner.

**Everyone will want communication without user identifiers — and only SimpleX network can provide it.**

* In 2021, 9% of 16+ year old US residents were victims of identity theft — [Bureau of Justice Statistics](https://bjs.ojp.gov/library/publications/victims-identity-theft-2021).
