# SimpleX Names Conditions of Use

You accept these Conditions of Use of SimpleX Names ("Names Conditions") by registering, receiving or extending a name in the .simplex namespace.

**SimpleX names**. A name is a record controlled by your key. The record can contain links to addresses in the SimpleX Network. "Community names" are marked in the registry as referring to specific communities, usually cities or territories. "Reserved names" are marked in the registry, also while registered. A reserved name cannot be extended, and only the registry's admin can register it, by assigning it, which ends the reservation.

**Registry**. The .simplex names registry is a set of contracts on the Ethereum blockchain. Name registrations, records, subnames and controlling addresses are stored in immutable contracts. Other contracts may be updated during a stabilization period, expected to last 12 to 24 months from the first registration.

**What the registry allows**.

- Except as the next point states, changing the record of the name, creating subnames and transferring control of the name require your key.
- A registration has a dispute period of 14 to 120 days, unless its registrant paid the registry for it in ETH in their own transaction or the registry's admin assigned the name; its end is stored in the registry. Within that period, also after a transfer, the registry's admin or guardian can end the registration without your key, together with its record and subnames. They end it only if it was made without a valid order for it, or a payment used for it is disputed, reversed or refunded in full. Unless the name is reserved, it can then be registered again at once.
- A name is registered for the registration period stored in the registry. Unless the name is reserved, it can be extended after its dispute period, if any, until 90 days after the registration period ends, at the price set at the time of extension. Once the registration period ends, control of the name cannot be transferred.
- If a payment used for the current registration of the name, or for its extension, is disputed, reversed or refunded in full, and that registration is not ended, the name can be reserved, so it cannot be extended.

**You agree**:

- not to use a name you control for fraud, to impersonate people or organizations, to store links to illegal content, or to knowingly infringe the rights of others, including trademarks;
- to use a community name you control to benefit that community, not as the brand of one person or business.

You are responsible for the names you control, their records and subnames. A name gives you no rights to the word itself, or to any word marks or other intellectual property.

**Your key**. Keep your key safe. If you lose your key, nobody can recover the key or control of the name, and nobody can reverse a transfer made with the key.

**Public information**. Everything in the registry is public with its history, including controlling addresses and acceptances of these Names Conditions.

**Changes to the conditions**. New versions of these Names Conditions are recorded in the registry. Each version applies to registrations, transfers and extensions made after it is recorded. A version that does not reduce your rights applies at once.
