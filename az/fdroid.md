### F-Droid.org repo

SimpleX Chat and F-Droid.org repositories sign builds with the different keys. To switch, please [export](https://simplex.chat/docs/guide/chat-profiles.html#move-your-chat-profiles-to-another-device) the chat database and re-install the app.
