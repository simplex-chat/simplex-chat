# Trail of Bits security audit 2026: findings and fixes

This page lists all findings of the [Trail of Bits security audit](https://github.com/simplex-chat/simplex-chat/blob/master/docs/SimpleX_Chat_Code_Review_2026_Final_Report_with_Fix_Review.pdf) of SimpleX Chat apps and the simplexmq library, the status assigned by Trail of Bits in their fix review, where and when we fixed each issue, and our comments. See the [blog post](./20261121-simplex-chat-security-audit-trail-of-bits-security-process.md) for the overview.

## Summary

Status in the report, as assessed by Trail of Bits in the fix review:

| Severity | Resolved | Partially resolved | Unresolved | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High | 4 | 2 | 0 | 6 |
| Medium | 11 | 3 | 2 | 16 |
| Low | 27 | 1 | 1 | 29 |
| Informational | 7 | 6 | 11 | 24 |
| **Total** | **49** | **12** | **14** | **75** |

After the fix review, we resolved two more issues in code: TOB-SMPLXCHAT-6 and 8.

## All findings

"Severity" and "Difficulty" are the ratings by Trail of Bits: the impact of the issue, and how hard it is to exploit. "Status" is the status in the Trail of Bits fix review. "Fix and release" lists each fix with the stable release of the apps or servers that first included it. All fixes are included in v7.1.4.
